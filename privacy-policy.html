Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

🔒 Workshop Profit does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. All your data stays on your device.

1. Overview

Workshop Profit ("the App") is a project management and profitability tracking tool for woodworkers and craftspeople. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. The short version: we collect nothing, we store nothing on our servers, and we share nothing with anyone.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Workshop Profit does not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, address)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavior data
  • Crash reports sent to our servers
  • Device identifiers
  • Payment information

3. Data Storage

All project data you enter — including project names, time logs, expenses, photos, and files — is stored locally on your device only. This data is:

  • Never uploaded to any server
  • Never accessible to us or any third party
  • Included in your device's standard iOS backup (iCloud or iTunes), if you have backups enabled

4. In-App Purchases

Workshop Profit offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive, process, or store any payment card information. Apple's privacy policy governs all transactions.

5. Photos and Files

If you attach photos or files to a project, these are stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to your photos or any attached files.

6. Third-Party Services

Workshop Profit does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not display ads.

7. Children's Privacy

Workshop Profit is rated 4+ on the App Store. It does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
