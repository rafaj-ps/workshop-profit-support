Frequently Asked Questions

Workshop Profit is a project management app for woodworkers and craftspeople. It helps you track time spent on each project, log material and supply costs, calculate your real profit, and understand your effective hourly rate — so you can price your work correctly.
The free version lets you create and fully use up to 2 projects, with access to all features: time tracking, expense logging, budget progress, analytics, photos, and file attachments. It's a complete trial — no features are locked, just the number of projects is limited.
Pro removes the 2-project limit so you can track unlimited projects. It's a single one-time purchase — no subscriptions, no recurring fees. Pay once and use the app forever.
Open Workshop Profit on your new device, go to the Pro upgrade screen, and tap "Restore Purchase." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used when you originally purchased. The restore is free and instant.
Yes. Go to Settings → Export Backup to save all your projects, photos and attachments to a file. On your new phone, use Settings → Restore from Backup. Your data is also included in your device's iCloud or iTunes backup automatically.
Effective Hourly Rate = (Selling Price − Total Expenses) ÷ Total Hours Worked. This gives you the real money you made per hour of your time, after covering all material costs.
