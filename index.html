What The App Does

Workshop Profit is an offline business tool for makers and workshop owners. It helps you:

  • Create projects for paid or private work
  • Track expenses and materials per project
  • Log time with a built-in timer
  • Set a selling price and see real profit instantly
  • Review analytics, export reports, and back up your data

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no registration, login, or cloud account required.

How does Pro work?

Workshop Profit Pro unlocks unlimited projects with a one-time non-consumable purchase. There is no subscription.

Can I restore my purchase?

Yes. Open the Pro screen and use Restore Purchase.

Privacy

Workshop Profit does not collect personal data or require a user account. Your project information is stored on your device.

Permissions Used

  • Camera: only when you choose to take a project photo
  • Photo Library: only when you choose to attach a project photo from your library
  • Files / Share Sheet: only when you export reports or backup/restore your data
  • Notifications: only for optional project reminders

Backup And Export

Exports

You can export project data as PDF or CSV from inside the app.

Backup

You can create a ZIP backup and later restore it on the same device or another device.

Important

Restore replaces the app’s current local data with the backup you import.

Troubleshooting

Store unavailable on the Pro screen

Check your internet connection and try again. If needed, reopen the app and retry the purchase screen.

Photo access not working

Check iPhone Settings and confirm Workshop Profit has permission for Camera or Photos.

Export or backup failed

Try again and make sure the iPhone share sheet opens fully before closing it.
